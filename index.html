Meet Christine

A practitioner who
listens first.

"Every person who walks through my door is carrying something. My job is to help them set it down."

Christine Webb has been practicing therapeutic massage and energy work for over 20 years — in luxury waterfront hotels, small bespoke studios, and now in her own private practice in Willards, Maryland.

She has a particular specialty in women's therapeutic massage and energy healing, with deep experience in how stress, hormonal shifts, and emotional holding patterns show up in the body. Her practice is open to anyone drawn to this kind of integrated, whole-person approach.

Christine works independently. You'll always see her — not a rotation of staff, not a different face each visit.

Common Questions

Good things to know.

Is this practice only for women?

Christine has a deep specialty in women's therapeutic massage and energy healing — including experience with how stress, hormonal shifts, and emotional patterns show up in the body. That said, her practice warmly welcomes anyone. If you're wondering whether it's a good fit, just give her a call.

I've never tried energy work. Where do I begin?

You don't need any prior experience — or even a clear sense of what you're looking for. Christine will talk with you about what's going on and help you find the right starting place. Many people begin with a massage and discover that energy work is a natural next step.

Can I combine services in one visit?

Absolutely. Many clients pair a massage with Reiki or a sound bath in a single session — it's a beautiful combination. Just mention what you're curious about when you reach out, and Christine can help you plan something that makes sense.

What should I wear?

Whatever is comfortable for you. For massage, you'll be warmly and appropriately draped throughout your session — you'll never feel exposed. For Reiki, sound bath, and shamanic work, you remain fully clothed the entire time.

Your Visit

How a session unfolds.

01
Arrive & settle in Come as you are — there's no need to prepare anything. You'll have a few quiet minutes to arrive, breathe, and leave the outside world at the door before anything begins.
02
A brief conversation Christine will check in with you — how your body is feeling, what's been weighing on you, what you're hoping to walk away with. You can share as much or as little as you'd like. She listens carefully and shapes everything from there.
03
Your session Every session is different because every person is different. Christine works intuitively, checking in as she goes, adjusting pressure and technique in real time. You are the guide — she follows your lead.
04
Take your time leaving There's no rush. After your session, you'll have space to rest, reorient, and ease back into yourself. Christine may offer a few suggestions for how to support the work in the days that follow.
